## 2. Ready-to-Copy Resume Project Section

```markdown
### ENTERPRISE PROJECT EXPERIENCE

**Project Apex: Financial ERP Modernization & S/4HANA Implementation**
*Role: Associate SAP FICO & Data Migration Analyst* | *Duration: 6 Months*
*Environment: SAP S/4HANA Finance (FICO 2022/2023), Tally.ERP 9, MS Excel, MS Word, MS PowerPoint*

* **Legacy Accounting Audit (Tally.ERP 9)**: Audited and extracted legacy financial masters, Chart of Accounts, open item sub-ledgers, and statutory tax registers (GST/TDS) for an automotive component manufacturer with ₹250 Cr turnover across 2 manufacturing plants.
* **Data Engineering & Cleansing (MS Excel)**: Designed comprehensive mapping workbooks using advanced formulas (`XLOOKUP`, `INDEX-MATCH`, `TRIM`, `PROPER`); eliminated duplicate vendor/customer entries by validating 1,800+ PAN and Indian GSTIN records prior to staging into SAP Migration Cockpit (LTMC).
* **Enterprise Structure & GL Configuration (SAP FICO)**: Configured core enterprise structure in SAP S/4HANA, including Company Code (`IN10`), Fiscal Year Variant (`V3`), Posting Period Variant (`PP10`), Field Status Variant (`FS10`), and unified Chart of Accounts (`COA1`).
* **S/4HANA Modern Architecture**: Configured Universal Journal (`ACDOCA`) with multi-ledger support (Leading Ledger `0L` for Local GAAP and Non-Leading `2L` for IFRS); enabled Document Splitting on Profit Center for real-time plant-level balance sheet generation.
* **Business Partner (BP) & Sub-ledger Integration**: Configured S/4HANA Business Partner model (Roles: `000000`, `FLVN00`, `FLVN01`, `FLCU00`, `FLCU01`), establishing automatic reconciliation accounts (`200100` Creditors, `100500` Debtors) to streamline Procure-to-Pay (P2P) and Order-to-Cash (O2C) cycles.
* **Payment Automation & Cash Management**: Configured Automatic Payment Program (`F110`/`FBZP`) for domestic banking integration, establishing payment terms (`NT15`, `NT30`), payment method `T` (Bank Transfer), and bank clearing sub-accounts.
* **Asset Accounting (New FI-AA)**: Configured Chart of Depreciation, Asset Classes for Plant & Machinery and IT assets, and depreciation keys (`WDV1`/`SLM1`) with real-time depreciation posting (`AFAB`).
* **Cutover Reconciliation & Audit Control**: Formulated cutover trial balance reconciliation models in Excel; validated zero-variance (`₹0.00`) between Tally closing balances and SAP loaded opening balances on cutover date across ₹41.75 Cr total balance sheet assets.
* **Business Documentation & Governance (MS Word & PPT)**: Authored 85-page Business Blueprint (BBP), Functional Specification (FS) for automated vendor payment advice dispatch, and End-User SOPs; designed executive milestone decks for Steering Committee presentations.
```
